**Surprise.** The Schlegel et al. 2024 figures (cell_type ≈98%, cell_class ≈43%) are reproduced by the *anatomical* central population (is_optic False: 97.9% and 43.5%). They are not reproduced by `super_class == central` (99.6% and 32.2%), which is the population the plan expected. Their "central brain" is a region-based population. This matters for D14: say which "central" is meant, because the two give different label coverage.

**Read-out for Project 1 (descriptive only, no classifier).**
- Sex-difference labels and unmatched types point to the same place: both are densest in higher-order regions.
- Unmatched does not mean sex-specific. The lowest match rate is in ascending neurons (55.5%), which more likely reflects naming and coverage at the brain–nerve-cord border.
- A sex claim must exceed the fafb–hemibrain difference (E6).

**Proposal for D18.** Pair left and right neurons within a type up to min(L, R). Keep every neuron of a type, paired or surplus, in the same split fold. Report surplus neurons separately rather than dropping them. A tolerance for "on the diagonal" should be agreed before any model is run. *Disclosure: this rule suits Project 1.*
